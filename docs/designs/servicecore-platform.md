## Vision

### 10x Check
Instead of dumping raw `axe-core` JSON warnings into generic LLM prose, ServiceCore turns automated web + PDF scans into an outcome-grade compliance & remediation engine (**Wave 1 CLI/pipeline `~15 Python files` `[estimated]`**; **Wave 2–3 Workbench/Stripe/Cloud Run `~25+ files` `[estimated]`**): (1) validated `before -> after` HTML/ARIA diffs & PDF tag actions with deterministic `rule_id -> (wcag_criterion, aoda_section)` legal citations (`EXP-01`), (2) tamper-evident canonical SHA-256 `evidence_digest` receipts binding `FindingEvidence` to human sign-off (`EXP-02`), and (3) a deterministic baseline diff engine (`NEW_REGRESSION`, `FIXED`, `PERSISTENT`) that powers the `$299/mo` monitoring tier (`EXP-03`).

### Platonic Ideal
Within 24 hours of onboarding, an SMB CTO or compliance lead receives a human-certified AODA/WCAG package: Page 1 is a signed conformance certificate with a verifiable SHA-256 `evidence_digest`; Pages 2+ group component-deduplicated findings with copy-pasteable PR diffs; and 30 days later `$299/mo` monitoring verifies fixes and catches new regressions.
